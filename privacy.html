Privacy

CarePlus stores account profiles, appointments, clinical records, prescriptions, payment records, notifications and messages in Supabase. Contact submissions are stored for clinic staff to respond.

Imported accounts and records originate from the clinic's existing CarePlus database. Patients can access their own records; clinic staff access records according to their assigned role.

Session credentials are stored in this browser to keep you signed in. Sign out when using a shared device.

Use Contact to request corrections or ask the clinic about retention, access or deletion. This page describes the application data flow; the clinic must supply its operating privacy policy and contact details before public launch.
