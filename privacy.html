🔒 Privacy Policy

How we protect your personal health information

📋 Introduction

CarePlus Smart Clinic Management Portal ("CarePlus", "we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy and personal health information. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect your data in accordance with the Personal Data Protection Act 2010 (PDPA) and other relevant Malaysian legislation.

By using our system, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

📝 Information We Collect

We collect the following types of information:

1. Personal Information

  • Full name and IC number
  • Email address and phone number
  • Date of birth and gender
  • Home address
  • Emergency contact details

2. Medical Information

  • Medical history and diagnoses
  • Symptoms and consultation notes
  • Prescriptions and medications
  • Allergies and blood type
  • Appointment records

3. Billing Information

  • Consultation charges
  • Payment status and history
  • Receipt records

Note: We do not store credit card information. All payments are processed manually at the clinic counter.

4. System Usage Information

  • Login credentials (encrypted)
  • System activity logs
  • Appointment check-in records (QR code scans)

🎯 How We Use Your Information

We use your information for the following purposes:

  • 🏥
    Medical Care: To provide healthcare services, maintain medical records, and support doctor-patient consultations
  • 📅
    Appointment Management: To schedule, confirm, and manage your clinic appointments
  • 💰
    Billing: To process payments and maintain billing records
  • 📧
    Communication: To send appointment reminders and respond to enquiries
  • 🔒
    System Security: To authenticate users and maintain system logs for security purposes
  • ⚖️
    Legal Compliance: To comply with PDPA 2010, Act 586, and MOH guidelines

🔒 How We Protect Your Data

Technical and organizational security measures to protect your personal health information

🔐

Encrypted Storage

Patient records and billing information are encrypted using AES-256 encryption standard to protect sensitive data at rest.

🔑

Secure Passwords

All passwords are hashed using bcrypt (minimum 10 rounds) and never stored in plaintext to prevent exposure.

🌐

HTTPS Protection

All data transmission between your browser and our server is encrypted via HTTPS protocol.

👥

Access Control

Role-based access control ensures users can only access information relevant to their role (Patient, Doctor, Admin).

✅

Input Validation

All user inputs are validated to prevent unauthorized data manipulation and maintain data integrity.

📋

Activity Logging

System activities are logged to support accountability and security monitoring.

👤 Your Privacy Rights (PDPA 2010)

Under the Personal Data Protection Act 2010, you have the following rights:

  • Right to Access: You can request access to your personal data held by the clinic
  • Right to Correction: You can request correction of inaccurate or incomplete personal data
  • Right to Withdraw Consent: You can withdraw consent for data processing, subject to legal and contractual restrictions
  • Right to Object: You can object to processing of your personal data in certain circumstances

To exercise any of these rights, please contact the clinic administrator at support@careplus.com or login to access your privacy settings.

📅 Data Retention

We retain your personal health information in accordance with:

  • 📊
    MOH Guidelines: Medical records are typically retained for at least 7 years from the date of last consultation
  • 🏥
    Act 586 Requirements: Private healthcare facilities must maintain proper records as required by law
  • ⚖️
    Legal Obligations: Data may be retained longer if required by law or for legal proceedings

After the retention period, data will be securely deleted or anonymized.

🔄 Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell or rent your personal information. We may share your data only in the following circumstances:

  • 👨‍⚕️
    Healthcare Providers: With doctors and medical staff involved in your care
  • ⚖️
    Legal Requirements: When required by law, court order, or government authority
  • 🚨
    Emergency Situations: To protect your vital interests or those of another person
  • ✅
    With Your Consent: When you have provided explicit consent for specific purposes

📋 Legal Compliance

CarePlus operates in accordance with the following Malaysian legislation and guidelines:

📜 PDPA 2010 Compliant
🏥 Act 586 Compliant
📊 MOH Guidelines

📜 Personal Data Protection Act 2010 (PDPA)

Malaysia's primary law governing the processing of personal data in commercial transactions. We comply with the seven principles of data protection under PDPA.

🏥 Private Healthcare Facilities and Services Act 1998 (Act 586)

Regulations governing private medical clinics, including standards for patient record-keeping and data management.

📊 Ministry of Health (MOH) Guidelines

Best practices for Electronic Medical Records (EMR) management, including data retention and security standards.

🚨 Data Breach Response

In the event of a data security incident that may compromise your personal information, we will:

  • 🔍
    Investigate the incident promptly and take immediate steps to contain it
  • 📢
    Notify affected users as soon as reasonably possible
  • 📋
    Provide information about the nature of the breach and data affected
  • 🏛️
    Report to relevant authorities as required by PDPA 2010
  • 🛡️
    Implement corrective measures to prevent future incidents

🔄 Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or legal requirements. We will notify users of significant changes by:

  • 📄
    Posting the updated policy on this page
  • 📅
    Updating the "Last Updated" date
  • 🔔
    Sending notifications through the system (for registered users)

Your continued use of CarePlus after changes are posted constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
